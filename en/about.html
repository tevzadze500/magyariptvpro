About us – the Magyar IPTV Pro team

We are a small Hungarian-speaking team focused on one thing: making Hungarian TV work smoothly on any device, anywhere. This page explains who we are, how we work, and what you can expect from us.

Who we are

Magyar IPTV Pro started in 2023, after years of struggling ourselves to watch Hungarian channels from abroad. The services we found were either unreliable or had no one who spoke Hungarian on support. So we built the thing we wanted to use.

Today we are a small technical and support team. No call centre, no chatbot: the person who answers you on WhatsApp is the same person who sets up your access.

Why we do it

Because the technical setup is the real barrier for most people. An M3U list or an Xtream Codes login is worthless if you don't know which app it goes into. That is why we wrote detailed guides for every popular player — from setting up TiviMate to activating IBO Player — and why we will activate your access for you if you ask.

How we work

  • Test first, pay later. We give you a 24-hour free trial so you can judge the real quality on your own connection.
  • We don't leave you alone with the setup. If you get stuck, we walk you through it or do it for you.
  • Real people, in Hungarian or English. Support is available 24/7 and our average reply time is a few minutes.
  • Transparent terms. Pricing, cancellation and refunds are written down in advance — see our refund policy.

What we don't promise

We won't claim nothing ever buffers. IPTV runs over the internet: your bandwidth, your Wi-Fi quality and your ISP's load all affect the picture. What we do promise is that when something breaks, we answer and we fix it. We keep a dedicated page for the most common problems in our troubleshooting guide.

Questions and contact

Most questions are already answered on our FAQ page. If you can't find what you need, reach us through the contact page, or browse all our IPTV setup guides.
